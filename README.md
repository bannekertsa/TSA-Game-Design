# Retro Revival Game Design: Neon Circuit Courier

## Theme Fit: Retro Revival
Neon Circuit Courier reimagines 8-bit/16-bit-era action-platformers with modern accessibility, combo-based movement, and bite-sized objectives. It features original characters, story, and music direction while evoking the feel of classic pixel-era arcade and console games.

## High Concept
You are a rookie courier in a neon-drenched, low-poly megacity that runs on a fragile energy grid. Your job is to deliver “power packets” to restore failing districts. Each level is a compact delivery route with hazards, secrets, and time-based scoring.

## Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Navigate** a side-scrolling route using tight jumps and wall interactions.
2. **Collect** power packets and optional data shards.
3. **Deliver** the packet to the district hub before the timer runs out.
4. **Score** is based on time, collectibles, and combo movement.

## Player Controls
- **Move**: Left/Right
- **Jump**: Single jump with a short coyote-time window (modern twist)
- **Dash**: Short burst in midair with cooldown
- **Interact**: Activate switches, terminals, and elevators

## Core Mechanics
- **Momentum Combo System (modern twist)**: Chain jumps, wall hops, and dashes to build a combo meter that boosts speed and score.
- **Energy Barriers**: Open with switches or by delivering a power packet to a substation.
- **Enemy Drones**: Simple movement patterns (patrol, hover, laser sweep) inspired by 16-bit AI, but fair and readable.
- **Secrets & Optional Routes**: Hidden vents and breakable panels for bonuses and alternate paths.

## Visual & Audio Direction
- **Visuals**: 16-bit-inspired pixel art with modern lighting overlays, parallax cityscapes, and bloom effects.
- **Audio**: Original chiptune synthwave soundtrack and crisp 8-bit-style SFX.

## Levels (At Least Three Playable Levels)
### Level 1: “Bootstrap Alley”
- **Purpose**: Tutorial stage.
- **Hazards**: Slow moving platforms, low-risk drones.
- **Gimmick**: Introduces switch-activated energy doors.
- **Goal**: Deliver a power packet to the alley substation.

### Level 2: “Skyline Relay”
- **Purpose**: Vertical platforming focus.
- **Hazards**: Wind gusts, moving cranes, midair drones.
- **Gimmick**: Multi-hop delivery—activate two substations before final hub opens.
- **Goal**: Time-based relay delivery with combo bonus.

### Level 3: “Circuit Core”
- **Purpose**: Advanced movement and timing.
- **Hazards**: Laser grids, collapsing floors, rapid drones.
- **Gimmick**: Split path—fast risky route vs. slower safe route.
- **Goal**: Deliver final packet to restore the city’s core.

## Accessibility & All-Ages Suitability
- **No mature themes** (no gore or adult content).
- **Adjustable difficulty**: Optional assist mode with slower hazards and longer timers.
- **Color-safe indicators** for switches and hazards.

## Scoring & Replayability
- **Time bonuses** and **combo multipliers** encourage mastery.
- **Collectible data shards** unlock cosmetic palette swaps.
- **Per-level leaderboard** for friendly competition.

## Story Summary (Little-Planet-Style Meaning)
A rookie courier team is tasked with restoring power to a city whose energy grid is failing. Each level is a district you help stabilize, culminating in the restoration of the central power core.

As you reconnect the city, the story reveals that the “power packets” are memories created by citizens: their hopes, kindness, and creativity. The courier’s journey is a metaphor for community care—when people share what they love, the whole city thrives. The final delivery restores not just electricity, but a sense of connection, reminding players that small acts can light up an entire world.

## Themes & Message
- **Creativity powers communities**: The city glows when people contribute their ideas and care.
- **Small actions matter**: Each delivery, no matter how small, helps everyone.
- **Cooperation beats isolation**: Side routes reward helping NPCs, showing that teamwork unlocks progress.

## Target Platforms
- PC and modern consoles
- Designed for controller and keyboard

## Compliance Checklist
- Inspired by 8-bit/16-bit era gameplay, visuals, and audio style.
- Original characters, story, and music direction.
- At least three playable, interactive levels.
- Suitable for all ages.
